## Brand & Style

This design system channels an unapologetic maximalist energy dedicated to human creative expression across visual arts, music, literature, sculpture, and performance. The brand persona is electric, intellectually adventurous, uninhibited, and celebratory. The target audience encompasses artists, collectors, patrons, and cultural explorers who view creative arts as an essential, sensory human experience rather than an academic relic.

The aesthetic fuses **Maximalism** with **Avant-Garde Constructivism** and expressive modernism:
- High-frequency visual layers, punchy color blocking, and intentional juxtaposition of rigid geometric frameworks with expressive organic curves.
- Tactile visual textures (halftone dot patterns, grain overlays, subtle paper noise, and collage-style framing) celebrating physical art mediums within digital spaces.
- Dynamic typographic scale contrasts that command attention, evoke artistic manifesto energy, and spark infectious creative enthusiasm.

## Colors

The color palette treats the interface as an artist's working palette—daring, highly saturated, and steeped in primary-derived avant-garde hues:

- **Primary (`#E62646` - Carmine Cadmium):** An electrifying, vibrant red evoking vitality, raw creative fire, and active cultural participation. Used for primary CTA buttons, highlighted markers, active badges, and assertive typographic accents.
- **Secondary (`#1B3B8A` - Ultramarine Blue):** A deep, resonant pigment blue providing anchoring contrast, structural authority, and framing against intense warm highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#FFAE00` - Radiant Marigold):** An unapologetic pop of chromatic joy, ideal for spotlight elements, category flags, badges, and kinetic hover responses.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8F4ED` - Warm Gesso Stone):** A tactile, warm canvas white reminiscent of heavy unprimed art paper, avoiding cold digital sterility.
- **Ink Black (`#111116`):** The stark, pitch contrast tone for crisp micro-copy, heavy structural borders, and high-impact silhouettes.

Color usage follows dynamic color-blocking rules: surfaces interact through deliberate contrast collisions (e.g., deep cobalt framing against marigold pills, with cadmium focal highlights).

## Typography

Typography delivers manifestos of excitement. The design system leverages condensed, tall, structural titleforms paired with an exceptionally clean, readable, humanist body sans, punctuated by geometric label systems:

- **Headlines (Bebas Neue):** Tall, commanding, and condensed proportions echo mid-century artistic exhibition posters and avant-garde broadsheets. Headlines always sit tight with low line heights, creating punchy textual blocks.
- **Body (Work Sans):** Highly legible, balanced, and structurally grounded to comfortably carry long-form essays, reviews, artist biographies, and discourse without competing with the headline energy.
- **Labels & UI Metadata (Space Grotesk):** Adds technical, geometric tension across dates, tags, event tickers, and catalog indices. Set with positive letter spacing and confident uppercase styling.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a **12-column responsive fluid grid** with intentional offsets, visual bleed zones, and collage framing:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 3rem margins, 1.5rem gutters. Enables asymmetric split screens, overlapping hero images, staggered cards, and expressive banner tickers that stretch edge-to-edge.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 2rem margins, 1.25rem gutters. Grid units collapse into dual-column editorial layouts with high visual impact.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, 1.25rem margins, 1rem gutters. Elements stack dynamically, transforming multi-axis art collections into rhythmic, high-contrast vertical streams.

Spacing rhythm deliberately alternates between dense clusters of metadata and expansive vertical pacing (`space-xl`), allowing critical artwork showcases to breathe while micro-interactions stay energetic and close-knit.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids soft, cloudy corporate drop shadows in favor of **graphic neo-brutalist depth** and **tonal layering**:

- **Hard Graphic Offsets:** Interactive elements and cards employ solid 3px to 5px drop shadows with 0px blur, rendered in solid `#111116` or vibrant `#1B3B8A`. This imparts a tangible, silkscreened, screen-printed physical feel.
- **Tonal Collage Stacking:** Depth is achieved by overlapping structural blocks with differing background saturations (e.g., a warm gesso stone container resting over an electric ultramarine band, cut through with marigold tags).
- **Thick Ink Outlines:** Crisp 1.5px to 2.5px solid ink-black borders delineate cards, frames, and interactive fields, preserving crisp graphic clarity amidst maximalist compositions.
- **Hover Transitions:** On hover, raised cards translate -3px vertically and -3px horizontally while their hard shadows expand proportionally, evoking responsive tactile tactility.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is crisp, disciplined, and punctuated with deliberate organic or geometric moments:

- **Primary Geometry:** Base corner radius is soft (`roundedness: 1` — 0.25rem to 0.5rem), providing a sharp, poster-like architectural structure without feeling painfully sharp.
- **Organic & Abstract Accents:** Badges, profile framing masks, and highlight callouts break conventions using asymmetrical cutouts, elliptical badges, pill contours for metadata tags, and circular stamp seals.
- **Contrast of Forms:** Rigid rectangular artwork tiles coexist alongside circular community avatars and wavy or serrated marquee ribbons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in `#E62646` with `#F8F4ED` uppercase label typography (`Space Grotesk`, bold). Framed by a 2px `#111116` border and a solid 4px hard black offset shadow. On click, translates down and right to simulate physical press.
- **Secondary:** Outlined with a 2px `#1B3B8A` or `#111116` border, background `#F8F4ED`, with a `#FFAE00` solid hard offset shadow.
- **Ghost/Text:** Uppercase with a high-contrast underline accent in `#FFAE00` that expands on hover.

### Cards
- **Art Showcase & Event Cards:** Constructed with a solid 2px ink border, background `#FFFFFF` or `#F8F4ED`, and a 4px hard drop shadow. Imagery sits flush to the top edge or inside high-contrast framed borders. Pinned tags in the top-right corner overlap the frame.

### Chips & Badges
- **Curatorial Tags:** Pill-shaped or asymmetric cut-corner chips in `#FFAE00` or `#1B3B8A` with bold uppercase typography. Perfect for art categories (e.g., "NEO-SURREALISM", "LIVE SALON").

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Thick 2px border, `#FFFFFF` background, solid 3px hard black offset shadow on focus with a `#E62646` active highlight border. Floating labels set in `Space Grotesk`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Custom geometric selectors with 2px borders and sharp/soft corners. Selected state bursts into solid `#E62646` with high-contrast `#F8F4ED` checkmarks.

### Dynamic Art-Community Additions
- **Exhibition Tickers:** Infinite scrolling horizontal marquees set in condensed typography across contrasting yellow/black or red/blue tape strips.
- **Collage Frame Lists:** Member discussions and critique feeds arranged in alternating left/right inset cards with decorative graphic stamp labels and reaction counters.
